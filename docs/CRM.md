# OptiFlow CRM

OptiFlow's CRM is a dedicated customer relationship workspace, separate from the accounting/ERP workflow.

## Customer 360

Each customer can be viewed with:

- identity and contact details
- latest invoice and balance
- invoice history
- prescription history and expiry
- loyalty history
- CRM activity timeline
- WhatsApp shortcut

## Activity workflow

Staff can create:

- Follow-up
- Call
- WhatsApp
- Visit
- Reminder
- Note

Activities can have a due date and move from OPEN to DONE/CANCELLED.

## CRM dashboard

The CRM dashboard surfaces:

- active customers
- new customers in the last 30 days
- open follow-ups
- overdue follow-ups
- prescriptions expiring in the next 30 days
- upcoming birthdays

## Production roadmap

The current CRM foundation is designed so the next layer can add:

1. customer tags and segments
2. lead/opportunity pipeline
3. automated birthday and prescription-expiry reminders
4. campaign history and delivery tracking
5. customer lifetime value and repeat-purchase analytics
6. staff ownership of CRM follow-ups
7. scheduled notifications
